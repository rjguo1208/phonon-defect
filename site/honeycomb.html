Toy 2: honeycomb lattice

A second toy model with two atoms per cell, like hexagonal boron nitride, and displacements in xxx, yyy and zzz. Updated 2026-10-02.

Why a second toy

The first toy validates the upfolding on a square lattice with one scalar displacement per site. It does not test what the first-principles calculation needs:

  • tensor force constants, 3×33\times33×3 blocks between atoms;
  • two atoms per cell and six branches, with gaps between them;
  • a flexural branch ωZA∝q2\omega_{\mathrm{ZA}}\propto q^2ωZA​∝q2, which exists only if the force constants obey the rotational sum rules;
  • a periodic host sampled on a q\mathbf qq-mesh, as from DFPT, from which the chain can be built in reciprocal space.

Model

A honeycomb lattice with bond length 1, lattice vectors a1=(3,0)\mathbf a_1=(\sqrt3,0)a1​=(3​,0) and a2=(3/2,3/2)\mathbf a_2=(\sqrt3/2,3/2)a2​=(3​/2,3/2), atom A at the origin and atom B at (0,1)(0,1)(0,1). Each atom moves in three directions. The harmonic energy is

with central springs on the nearest-neighbour A–B bonds (k1k_1k1​) and on the second-neighbour A–A and B–B bonds (k2k_2k2​), and a bending term for every atom that measures how far it sits above or below the plane of its three neighbours. The parameters are mA=1m_A=1mA​=1, mB=1.3m_B=1.3mB​=1.3 (close to the boron-to-nitrogen mass ratio), k1=1k_1=1k1​=1, k2=0.2k_2=0.2k2​=0.2 and κ=0.3\kappa=0.3κ=0.3, chosen so that the ratio of the ZO to the TO frequency at Γ\GammaΓ is 0.63, close to that of h-BN.

  • Sum rules hold exactly. Every term is unchanged by the three rigid translations and the three rigid rotations, so the acoustic and the rotational sum rules hold term by term.
  • The flexural branch is quadratic. A displacement uzu^zuz that is linear in position costs no bending energy, because the three neighbours of an atom are centred on it.
  • In-plane and out-of-plane motion decouple. The mirror plane z→−zz\to-zz→−z splits the 6×66\times66×6 dynamical matrix into a 4×44\times44×4 in-plane block and a 2×22\times22×2 out-of-plane block.
  • Defects. A substitution changes the mass of one atom and multiplies every energy term that contains it by a common factor, so the sum rules still hold exactly and need not be imposed afterwards, unlike force constants spliced from a supercell.

Checks

tests/test_honeycomb.py runs six tests in about a second:

  • every energy term annihilates the six rigid motions to 10−1410^{-14}10−14 (acoustic and rotational sum rules);
  • D(q)D(\mathbf q)D(q) is Hermitian, its in-plane and out-of-plane blocks do not couple, the two phase conventions give the same eigenvalues, and there are exactly three zero frequencies at Γ\GammaΓ;
  • ωZA/q2\omega_{\mathrm{ZA}}/q^2ωZA​/q2 and ωTA/q\omega_{\mathrm{TA}}/qωTA​/q are constant for qqq from 0.002 to 0.008, to 10−310^{-3}10−3 and 10−410^{-4}10−4;
  • no negative ω2\omega^2ω2 on a 30×3030\times3030×30 grid;
  • the spectrum of a periodic 5×55\times55×5 supercell equals the Bloch spectrum on the 5×55\times55×5 grid to 10−1210^{-12}10−12;
  • a substitution keeps exactly three zero modes and the uniform translations, and a light substitution (M′=0.4M'=0.4M′=0.4, all its terms ×1.3\times1.3×1.3) has a mode above the top of the host spectrum.

Next: defects and the chain

  • Defects. A light substitution on A, expected to give local modes above 1.86, modes in the gap 1.36–1.55, and out-of-plane modes in the ZA–ZO gap at K that the mirror symmetry keeps from mixing with in-plane motion. A heavy substitution, expected to give in-band resonances, among them a flexural one at low frequency. A vacancy, which removes three coordinates.
  • Cluster. The atoms within a few bonds of the defect; it contains every changed term.
  • Chain from the Bloch modes. On an L×LL\times LL×L q\mathbf qq-mesh the host is diagonal in q\mathbf qq. Block Lanczos on the whole host, started from the cluster coordinates, then gives the cluster block first and the bath chain after it, without a real-space bath matrix. This is the reciprocal-space route planned for MoS₂.
  • Gates. With the chain built on the same L×LL\times LL×L mesh, the upfolded matrix must reproduce the defect supercell exactly, because the chain terminates. On larger meshes it should converge to the dilute limit. Further gates: local, gap and resonant modes against exact solutions, and convergence of the chain at low frequency, where the flexural branch has a constant density of states.

Reproduce

The model is phdef/honeycomb.py. npm run honeycomb (python3 scripts/honeycomb_figures.py) writes Figure 1 and its CSV files in a few seconds, and python -m pytest tests runs the checks above with the other unit tests.
