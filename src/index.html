Electrons scattered by defects and phonons

Point defects at dilute concentration, phonons at finite temperature, and the cross terms between the two. Updated 2026-10-01.

Status: method designed; the phonon upfolding is implemented and validated on a toy lattice; the first-principles MoS₂ calculations are planned and have not been run.

Problem

In a crystal with a dilute concentration \(n_d\) of point defects, an electron near a band edge scatters elastically from the static defect potential and inelastically from phonons. The usual practice adds the two rates (Matthiessen's rule),

\[\frac{1}{\tau_{\mathbf k}}\approx\frac{1}{\tau^{\mathrm{ph}}_{\mathbf k}}+n_d\,\frac{1}{\tau^{\mathrm{el}}_{\mathbf k}} .\]

This project computes the cross terms of order \(n_d\,g^2\) that the sum leaves out, with the defect potential kept to all orders (T-matrix) and the electron–phonon coupling \(g\) to lowest order:

  • phonon emission and absorption enhanced by a defect resonance, when the electron passes through a quasi-bound state while it scatters a phonon;
  • inelastic scattering from vibrations localized at the defect (local and resonant modes);
  • the change of the electron–phonon vertex near the defect;
  • interference between the defect and phonon scattering amplitudes, which violates Matthiessen's rule.

Idea

Lihm and Park (arXiv:2409.07393) turn the frequency-dependent plasmon–phonon problem into a frequency-independent matrix by adding the plasmon as an explicit auxiliary mode. We make the same move for defects, with one difference. Harmonic defect phonons are already static; frequency dependence appears only when the infinite host is folded onto a cluster around the defect. The auxiliary modes are therefore the sites of a block Lanczos chain of the host:

\[\tilde C=\begin{pmatrix}\mathcal D_{cc}&B_0^{\dagger}E_1^{\dagger}\\ E_1B_0&L_m\end{pmatrix},\qquad D_{cc}(\omega)=\Big[\big((\omega+i0^+)^2-\tilde C\big)^{-1}\Big]_{cc} .\]

One diagonalization of \(\tilde C\) gives the defect-dressed phonon modes at every frequency. Electrons couple to them through static vertices, and the electron scattering states come from the electron–defect T-matrix. The derivation is on the Method page.

Repository

  • phdef/: block Lanczos chain, upfolded matrix, cluster Green function by mode sum and by continued fraction, damped companion matrix.
  • examples/square_lattice_toy.py: the toy validation.
  • tests/: unit tests, run with python -m pytest tests.
  • reference/lihm_park_2024/: numerical checks of the Lihm–Park identities.
  • src/ and site/: this website. LaTeX is rendered at build time with KaTeX (npm run build, then npm run check).

Earlier work in this repository

Until 2026-07-01 this repository held the phonon-side T-matrix of monolayer MoS₂ with a sulfur vacancy and with an oxygen substitution, built from finite-displacement force constants and a cluster spectral density, together with notes on the electron–defect T-matrix. That work is preserved at the tag archive/2026-07-01. Its phonon results, for example the OS local modes at 59.2 and 66.2 meV above the host spectrum, are the phonon-side truth gates of the Plan.
